What is Magbabaya to the Obu Manuvu?
A) A forest guard
B) The Supreme Being
C) A tribal healer
D) A river spirit
ANSWER: B

What do the Obu Manuvu call disease epidemics?
A) Pusaka
B) Iliyan
C) Pama
D) Uyon
ANSWER: D

How does the pama ritual detect disasters?
A) Reading river color
B) Counting passing birds
C) Watching hanging firewood
D) Reading burned smoke
ANSWER: C

What are iliyan?
A) Natural safe havens
B) Volunteer forest guards
C) Spiritual warning conduits
D) Communal labor groups
ANSWER: A

What does Pusaka mainly involve?
A) Labor exchange
B) Cultural conservation
C) Riverbank evacuation
D) Epidemic rituals
ANSWER: B

What does Matigsalug mean?
A) People of the mountain
B) People of the forest
C) People of the river
D) People of the elders
ANSWER: C

What is the Matigsalug customary law called?
A) Pasagi
B) Hungos
C) Kaingin
D) Baylan
ANSWER: A

Which sign warns the Matigsalug of floods?
A) Dropping well levels
B) Swaying hanging firewood
C) Silent morning birds
D) Fish jumping excessively
ANSWER: D

What does brown, fast-flowing river water tell the Matigsalug?
A) A drought is coming
B) Upstream rain risks landslides
C) Fishing will be good
D) An epidemic is near
ANSWER: B

What is hungos?
A) Mutual labor sharing
B) Swidden farming method
C) A spiritual ritual
D) An evacuation shelter
ANSWER: A
